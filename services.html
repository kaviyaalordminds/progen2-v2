Frequently Asked Questions On Solar Panel & Energy Solutions Services

A solar module, commonly known as a solar panel, is made up of photovoltaic cells that convert sunlight into electricity. When sunlight hits these cells, they generate direct current (DC) electricity through a process called the photovoltaic effect. This energy can be used immediately or stored for later use. Solar modules are a renewable, efficient, and eco-friendly solution, turning abundant sunlight into usable power and paving the way for a more sustainable future.

A solar inverter performs an electrifying transformation. It converts DC electricity from solar panels into AC power, the lifeblood of household appliances. This coveted AC power is also a perfect match for the electrical grid, harmonizing with ease.

Solar pumps use sunlight to power water pumps. They turn sunlight into energy to move water. These pumps are mainly used in rural and off-grid areas to supply water for farming, livestock, and drinking. They are affordable and efficient and provide a reliable water source without electricity. Solar pumps help ensure that communities in need have access to water.

At night, LED streetlights powered by solar energy brighten our streets. These lights capture sunlight during the day to illuminate public spaces after dark. Cities benefit from lower energy costs and a smaller carbon footprint. Solar lighting is a smart and sustainable solution, reducing the need for traditional power sources. These eco-friendly lights are helping create a greener future, transforming urban spaces with every glowing bulb.

Solar panels outlast other system parts. Modules last 25-30 years, as they harness sunlight. Inverters need replacing sooner, after 10-15 years. Energy storage technology varies most, with batteries lasting 5-15 years depending on type and use. Routine care extends each component's lifespan, maximizing the system's overall efficiency and durability.
