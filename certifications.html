Certifications

We're proud to hold ISO 9001:2015 certification from TÜV AUSTRIA, the globally respected standard for Quality Management Systems. This certification validates what our clients already know: we take quality seriously, from the first engineering sketch to the final panel installation.

What this means for you

Our ISO 9001:2015 certification covers the full scope of our solar EPC work—Engineering, Procurement, and Installation of Solar Power Plants. But beyond the certificate on the wall, this standard shapes how we actually run our business:

  • Reliable delivery. You can count on consistent, high-quality solar solutions that meet rigorous standards every single time.
  • Smarter processes. Our projects follow structured, well-defined workflows that keep things on track and minimize surprises.
  • Genuine partnership. We focus on real customer satisfaction and building trust with everyone we work with—not just meeting minimum requirements.
  • Forward-thinking risk management. We actively identify potential issues before they become problems, and we're always looking for ways to improve.
  • Full compliance. We stay current with all relevant regulations and industry requirements so you don't have to worry about them.
  • Sustainable operations. Efficiency and reliability aren't buzzwords for us; they're principles that drive how we grow responsibly.
